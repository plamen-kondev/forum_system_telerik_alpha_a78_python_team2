How it works

  1. Post an opinion in one of the categories.
  2. Others reply with arguments for or against it.
  3. Everyone votes on the arguments, not on the opinion.
  4. The author marks the reply that convinced them most.

House rule

Challenge opinions, not people. Personal attacks get removed and repeat offenders get blocked.
